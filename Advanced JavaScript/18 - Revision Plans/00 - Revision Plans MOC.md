# Revision Plans MOC

This module schedules repetition so the rest of the vault stays available under pressure: it packages the whole learning path into checklists, day-by-day plans, and condensed drill sets. Pick one plan (7-day or 14-day), follow it with written outputs every day, and use the master checklist and drill files to prove readiness rather than passively re-reading notes. Checking a box means you can explain, trace, and fix — not that you recognize the term.

## You're Done When

- [ ] You completed the 7-day or 14-day plan with at least one written output per day.
- [ ] You solved at least 15 code-output questions total, predicting before running.
- [ ] You explained at least 7 production bugs with root cause, fix, and tradeoff.
- [ ] You completed at least one full mock interview.
- [ ] Your weak-area log contains exact rules and fixes, not vague topic names.
- [ ] You passed the Final Readiness Test: 5 concepts at two depths, 5 unseen output questions, 2 scenarios with verification plans.
- [ ] You redid every weak-area question 24 hours later and got it right.
