# 06 - Front End System Design Cheatsheet

This is a quick reference summary of the most important things to remember during your front end system design interviews.

## 1. RADIO Framework
The structure to follow when answering prompts:
- **R**equirements Exploration: Understand the problem, gather functional/non-functional requirements, ask clarifying questions.
- **A**rchitecture / High-level design: Identify key components (View, Store, Data Access) and their relationships.
- **D**ata model: Describe entities, fields, and where they belong (Client vs Server).
- **I**nterface definition (API): Define how components communicate (HTTP, WebSockets, Props, Store Actions).
- **O**ptimizations and deep dive: Focus on performance, UX, accessibility, and domain-specific challenges.

## 2. Evaluation Axes
What interviewers are grading you on:
- **Problem exploration**: Did you clarify ambiguities?
- **Architecture**: Did you define clear responsibilities and break the problem down?
- **Technical proficiency**: Do you understand front end domain fundamentals (Performance, a11y, networking, i18n)?
- **Exploration and tradeoffs**: Did you suggest various approaches, explain pros/cons, and make a recommendation?
- **Product and UX sense**: Did you consider the end-user experience?
- **Communication and collaboration**: Did you communicate clearly and partner with the interviewer?

## 3. Common Mistakes to Avoid
- **Jumping in immediately:** Always ask questions and gather requirements first.
- **Unstructured answers:** Going all over the place. Use RADIO to stay grounded.
- **"One True Solution":** Insisting there's only one right answer. Acknowledge tradeoffs!
- **Silence:** Do not think purely in your head. Think out loud.
- **Going down rabbit holes:** Don't spend all your time on a deep dive before establishing the high-level architecture.
- **Using buzzwords you can't explain:** Only use terms you deeply understand.
